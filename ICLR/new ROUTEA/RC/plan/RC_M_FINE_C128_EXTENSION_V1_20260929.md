## Why upstream attribution remains open

Deleting P proves dependence on a whole input path, not which property supplies identity discrimination. Previous phase interventions can alter both P's internal relations and its registration with A/J. The completed nine-group registration experiment increased target and wrong logM similarly; it did not establish a reliable increase in their relative gap. A change in support level therefore cannot be equated with preferential identity evidence.

The existing full-C128 context chain separately tests P-only shifts, A/J shifts and common shifts. Its original11-query cohort and sealed implementation remain unchanged. This extension closes missing original-event coverage for phase/amplitude; it does not itself complete context attribution. Matched grid controls constrain, but do not remove, decoder boundary and nonlinear interaction explanations. No unique-cause claim is prespecified.
