## 3. Frozen populations and fresh-D1 base

The successor preserves two disjoint populations:

- Pair64: 64 registered pair-training queries in sealed order;
- current64: the frozen TRAIN32 and EVAL32 queries, ordered by registered
  execution ordinal within role.

Each record joins by unique `query_id` to the canonical 987-query ledger and
then to exactly one OOF record. Only the canonical ledger supplies
`query_ordinal` and `heldout_fold`. Historical `inner_fold`, historical
candidate positions and historical target/competitor pairs are not routing or
candidate authorities.

For each query, preserve from the sealed fresh-D1 OOF record:

- all 5,413 physical-row scores;
- the corrected 5,412-identity ranking;
- the natural top-128 identities and representative physical rows;
- the fresh-D1 winner, ranking and base gaps;
- native float32 adapted query tokens and hashes;
- the held-out checkpoint binding.

The candidate axis contains exactly 128 distinct corrected identities in
fresh-D1 ranking order. D1 adapts query tokens only; reference tokens remain
frozen. Target insertion, candidate widening, RAW-axis carryover, old-D1-axis
carryover and historical winner/slot carryover are forbidden.

The historical Pair64 V2 and current64 full-negative artifacts fix membership,
role, order and feature semantics only. Their old base scores, C128 axes, maps
and feature tensors are not fresh-D1 numerical inputs.

## 4. Target-free prejoin rematerialization

Before any target/role join, the implementation must seal a query-ID-only
Pair64/current64 roster. If a historical container is deserialized, receipts
must distinguish artifact access from semantic consumption. Poisoning its
target identity, switch label, target/competitor position, historical C128 or
historical fold must leave every prejoin output unchanged.

### 4.1 Fresh reference union

Derive the required physical-row union only from the fresh-D1 C128 axes. The
historical 3,004-row reference cache is incomplete for this new axis and cannot
be used as the union authority. An existing row may be byte-reused only when
physical row, source image, preprocessing frame, grid, tokens and source
receipt replay exactly. Every missing row must be resolved through the frozen
reference resolver; no row may be dropped or substituted.

Reference-union construction is target-free and must be independently sealed.

### 4.2 Fixed three arms

For normalized fresh-D1 query tokens `q`, frozen candidate-reference tokens
`r_g`, their shared similarity matrix `M_g`, and the registered
candidate-specific proposal maps `wq_g` and `wr_g`, preserve exactly:

- **A ALL**: query weights one, reference weights one;
- **B QUERY**: `wq_g` on query and reference weights one, i.e.
  `query-region x full-reference`;
- **C PAIRED**: `wq_g` on query and `wr_g` on reference, i.e.
  `query-region x paired-reference-region`.

Use the same candidate-specific maps, similarity matrix, normalization, score
formula, canonical geometry, fixed RoMa-v2 checkpoint, roll controls and
candidate population for all arms. Only the stated mask differs. Preserve the
registered six native feature semantics. Do not scan pooling, temperature,
top-k, area, map threshold, affine rule or rival rule.

For every query-candidate-arm record, save sufficient tensors and hashes for
independent replay of REAL score, visibility mass, score/mass, query-roll
response, reference-roll response, both maps and their geometries.

### 4.3 Candidate controls

Candidate reorder and C_BIND are different controls:

- candidate reorder jointly permutes candidate keys and all associated base
  and local payloads; restoring order must reproduce the original output;
- C_BIND leaves the query, destination candidate key, destination fresh-D1
  base score and base winner fixed, while destination position `i` receives
  the complete local evidence bundle from `(i+64) mod 128`.

C_BIND moves score, visibility mass, score/mass and both roll-response
features. It is fixed-point-free, instantiated on each fresh natural C128,
performed before challenger selection, and excluded from training. It cannot
be a target/rival swap, label rename or after-selection score replacement.

## 6. Post-seal label join

Only after the complete target-free aggregate is independently sealed may a
separate process read frozen targets and TRAIN/EVAL roles. It cannot modify
candidate membership, maps, scores or features.

For each Pair64 query:

- target is the D1 winner: strongest wrong is a negative HOLD;
- target is naturally in C128 but is not winner: target is the positive
  challenger;
- target is absent: strongest non-winner is a negative HOLD.

There is no target insertion or result-dependent rebalance. All Pair64 rows
remain in sealed order. TRAIN32 may later supply registered full-negative
episodes; EVAL32 cannot enter training, standardization or threshold choice.

Every deployed path must score all 127 non-winners and choose its own strongest
challenger. A target-versus-given-rival assay, RAW rival or oracle pair cannot
substitute. HOLD preserves the full base ranking; the one permitted SWITCH
moves the selected challenger to rank one and shifts only candidates that
previously preceded it.

Target-present summaries may be reported, but full EVAL32 deployment metrics
retain target-absent queries.

The only successful statuses are:

- producer:
  `ROUTEA_N2_FRESH_D1_MATCHED_THREE_ARM_POSTSEAL_JOIN_READY`;
- validator:
  `ROUTEA_N2_FRESH_D1_MATCHED_THREE_ARM_POSTSEAL_JOIN_VALIDATED`.

Only the validated join may authorize creation of
`N2_FRESH_D1_MATCHED_THREE_ARM_SEVEN_PARAMETER_CROSSFIT_CONTRACT`. This
contract does not construct an optimizer or train a head.

3. rescues exceed breaks and breaks are at most one;
