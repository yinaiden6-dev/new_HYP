# DINO-RCDE Track R V124 corrected-mechanism authority plan (draft V1)

Date: 2026-08-24  
Status: `DRAFT_ONLY_NOT_CURRENT_AUTHORITY`  
Stage: `TRACK_R_V124_CORRECTED_MECHANISM_PLANNING`  
Scientific claim: none  
Natural execution, training, submission, target join, and automatic continuation: not authorized

## 1. Purpose and authority boundary

This document is an additive plan for a successor to V123. It does not mutate,
replace, validate, or authorize any existing source, runtime, result, checkpoint,
launcher, or registry entry. In particular, it is not
`registry/current_authority_v124_*.json` and cannot be used as an execution
authority.

The successor has four separate jobs:

1. repair `C_DINO_V` so its donor population is the complete natural C128 axis;
2. make `CW1_QUERY_MULTITILE_FULL_REFERENCE` use the same ordered-root decode and
   overlap reducer as `CW1_QUERY_MULTITILE_LOCAL_COMPONENT_SET`;
3. restore the omitted pre-P `C_COL_P` causal stage; and
4. qualify a generalization-aware V retraining procedure by nested,
   identity-and-supergroup-disjoint inner validation.

These jobs must not be collapsed into one before/after number. The mechanism-only
repair and the retraining change require separate receipts and separate
interpretations.

The eventual V124 authority must byte-bind at minimum:

- the highest current authority and every later additive repair that remains in
  force;
- the CW1 query-multitile correction addendum;
- the V123 result, independent validation, prejoin shards, and postjoin metadata;
- the full-C128 P-V2 source, strict-lock, geometry, corrected-identity, DINO cache,
  fold, and candidate-axis artifacts actually consumed by V124;
- the new producer, independent validator, tests, launchers, and complete import
  closure; and
- an explicit development-versus-confirmation data-role registry.

Any hash, role, address, candidate-axis, fold, identity, supergroup, mask,
checkpoint, or reducer drift is an engineering abort. It cannot be converted to
a scientific NO-GO.

## 2. Additive forensic disposition of V123

V123's files remain immutable historical artifacts. A V124 authority must add a
machine-readable forensic disposition with the following meaning:

```text
V123_PROTOCOL_ABORT_PENDING_CONTROL_AND_ARM_REPAIR
```

The disposition is required because the V123 scorer passed only the two anonymous
pair members to a helper whose contract requires the complete natural C128 axis,
and because the V123 FULL and LOCAL arms changed decoder granularity as well as
reference scope. V123 also omitted `C_COL_P` from its scored/reduced control
population.

Therefore V123's `decision_A`, `spatial_boundary`, and `decision_B` are not
consumable as formal causal scientific decisions. The numerical outputs remain
available only as diagnostics of the observed checkpoint and implementation.
This reclassification does not imply either that the corrected mechanism will
pass or that all of the V123 REAL-path failure was caused by the implementation
defects.

## 3. Data roles: the 594 queries are opened diagnostics

All 594 target-hit queries used by V123 have now undergone target/rival join,
scientific reduction, per-arm inspection, and implementation-focused diagnosis.
They are permanently opened development data for V124.

They may be used only for:

- exact old-versus-repaired implementation replay;
- synthetic and natural engineering canaries;
- debugging receipt, parity, numerical, and resource failures;
- estimating whether a repaired path is worth freezing for confirmation; and
- nested development of a training policy whose final claim is evaluated
  elsewhere.

They may not be used for:

- a new paper-level GO or NO-GO;
- confirmatory p-values, confidence intervals, gate claims, or sealed-endpoint
  language;
- choosing a mechanism, loss, checkpoint grid, donor count, threshold, pooling,
  scale, or early-stopping rule and then evaluating that choice as if untouched;
  or
- claiming full-gallery retrieval, ownership, target presence, HOLD/SWITCH, or
  generalization to new identities.

The corrected implementation may report a status such as
`V124_OPENED594_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE`; its scientific decision fields must be
null. A later confirmatory authority requires a newly frozen endpoint whose
queries, identities, and supergroups were not used by V123 or by V124 mechanism
or training selection. If no such endpoint exists, V124 must stop at an
engineering/diagnostic result.

## 4. Corrected `C_DINO_V`: complete C128 before pair scoring

### 4.1 Population and intervention

For each target-free query, V124 must first materialize the canonical natural
C128 destination axis in physical-row order. Each destination must include its
own immutable P lock and its own complete DINO candidate field. Only after all
128 candidates are present may V124 construct a deterministic,
corrected-identity-disjoint perfect matching of DINO reference donors.

The control changes only the destination-to-DINO-reference-content binding:

```text
destination candidate key / physical row / P lock
    + identity-disjoint donor's complete native DINO token, grid, valid-mask,
      source, cache, and geometry payload
```

It must not rerun P, change the query, change the candidate axis, insert or drop a
candidate, resize donor tokens, or read a target, rival role, rank, score, winner,
gap, outcome, opened, or sealed datum.

For the anonymous pair that is later decoded, neither member may receive DINO
content from either member of that pair. This additional fixed matching
constraint prevents the control from degenerating into a two-member pair swap.
If a complete identity-disjoint matching satisfying this constraint does not
exist, the query is a preregistered control-ineligible engineering state; the
producer must not fall back to a two-way swap or a smaller donor axis.

### 4.2 Mandatory receipt and independent validation

Every query-level receipt must prove:

- `candidate_count == 128` and canonical positions `0..127`;
- destination and donor sequences are complete permutations of the same C128
  axis;
- zero fixed points and corrected donor identity different from destination
  identity at every position;
- the two scored anonymous pair members do not donate to either scored member;
- input/output native DINO-content multisets are byte-identical;
- all destination P-lock object hashes, query bytes, member addresses, model
  checkpoint, decoder, reducer, dtype, and candidate-axis hash are unchanged;
- no target/rival role or result was read while constructing the mapping; and
- donor mapping and scores are deterministic under an independent reconstruction.

The validator must reconstruct the C128 axis and donor matching from upstream
artifacts. Checking only producer-declared booleans or receipt hashes is
insufficient. A unit sentinel must fail if the caller supplies only two locks,
even when those two locks can be deranged.

An individual repaired control score is allowed to equal `-REAL` by numerical
coincidence, but a structural equality caused by pair swapping is forbidden and
must be detected before reduction.

## 5. Corrected FULL arm: rootwise matched decoding

For every owner hypothesis, both regional arms must enumerate the same ordered
root population. For each comparable root `u`, both arms call the same decoder on
the same query-root mask and in the same order:

```text
FULL(u):  q[T_u] x full_valid(ref_owner) x full_valid(ref_opponent)
LOCAL(u): q[T_u] x R[owner,u]          x R[opponent,u]
```

Both arms must then apply the same root eligibility, exact missing-root zero,
overlap-count tensor, reciprocal overlap weights, unique-query-union mask,
fixed denominator, signed patch accumulation, direction reducer, pair reducer,
and dtype policy. The only permitted difference between FULL and LOCAL is the two
reference masks passed for each root.

The FULL arm must not decode `query_union x full_reference` once, because
assignment, consensus, and dual-softmax are nonlinear. A single union call and a
rootwise call are different mechanisms, not interchangeable implementations.

Independent validation must byte-compare FULL and LOCAL receipts for:

- ordered root ordinals and per-root query-mask hashes;
- root READY/H0/missing decisions;
- overlap-count and reciprocal-weight tensors;
- union mask, fixed denominator, candidate pair, direction, checkpoint,
  adapter/decoder/reducer, and dtype; and
- forward-call count and root-to-call mapping.

It must separately prove that only owner/opponent reference-mask hashes differ.

The repaired FULL-versus-LOCAL contrast may support a reference-scope claim.
FULL-versus-ALL_PATCH remains a matched mechanism comparison, but it is not a
strict single-variable query-mask causal effect unless a later contract also
matches their decoder granularity. A positive regional increment over ALL_PATCH
must therefore be described as an increment, not proof that query scope alone
caused it.

## 6. Restore `C_COL_P` as a separate pre-P stage

### 6.1 Causal question

`C_COL_P` asks whether the ColNomic proposal path is candidate-bound. It is not a
post-hoc P-lock shuffle and is not interchangeable with `C_DINO_V`:

```text
C_COL_P: candidate binding intervention -> rebuild P features -> rerun frozen P
         head/resolver -> reseal Q/root/component locks -> run unchanged DINO V

C_DINO_V: preserve P locks -> replace only DINO reference content -> run V
```

The existing canonical C_COL implementation and its historical I1 preflight are
useful implementation evidence, but they do not prove compatibility with the
current strict P-V2 source/head/lock lineage. V124 may consume that implementation
only after a new compatibility and independent-replay gate binds the exact P-V2
artifacts used by V124.

### 6.2 Full-C128 rebuild

For every query, before P is run:

1. reconstruct the canonical 128-candidate ColNomic source axis;
2. choose one deterministic, complete, fixed-point-free, corrected-identity-
   disjoint donor permutation without reading labels or results;
3. bind each donor's complete native ColNomic reference tokens, valid mask,
   native grid, root/action bank, and deployment source to the destination;
4. recompute every destination/direction P feature using the unchanged query;
5. run the same fold-local P-V2 outer-refit checkpoint, feature equations,
   deployability mapping, resolver, tie break, H0 rule, and lock sealer; and
6. join the rebuilt locks back to the original destination C128 DINO axis and run
   the byte-identical frozen V checkpoint.

Donor ColNomic tokens may not be resized. Donor deployment masks may be mapped to
the destination DINO grid only through the frozen positive-area geometry rule;
illegal mapped components become exact H0 with no retry or fallback. The DINO
query and all 128 DINO candidate fields must remain byte-identical to REAL.

### 6.3 Interpretation by arm

- `ALL_PATCH_SAME_MODEL` does not consume P and must be byte-identical between
  REAL and `C_COL_P`. It is an invariance check, not a destruction gate.
- `CW1_QUERY_MULTITILE_FULL_REFERENCE` may change only because the rebuilt P seal
  changes the candidate-bound query hypothesis/root population. A drop supports
  candidate binding in the proposal/query-hypothesis path, not DINO local
  reference binding.
- `CW1_QUERY_MULTITILE_LOCAL_COMPONENT_SET` consumes both rebuilt query roots and
  rebuilt local reference components. Together with a valid `C_DINO_V` drop it
  is required for the full ColNomic-propose/DINO-verify causal chain.

Because `C_COL_P` rebuilds the whole proposal, it does not by itself identify
whether a drop arose from Q, root choice, or R. More specific controls are needed
for such a decomposition.

### 6.4 C_COL preflight and validation gate

Before any natural V forward, a dedicated C_COL stage must emit and independently
validate, for all 594 opened diagnostics (or an explicitly smaller fixed
engineering canary before full diagnostic execution):

- 128 destinations, two directions, and exactly 256 rebuilt lock records per
  eligible query;
- complete donor permutation, zero fixed points, identity disjointness, input/
  output ColNomic-content multiset equality, and zero response-dependent retry;
- exact destination C128/DINO/query/checkpoint/candidate-axis preservation;
- exact P-V2 checkpoint, feature, resolver, tie-break, H0, geometry, and lock
  schema lineage;
- an independently reconstructed donor map and a deterministic replay of every
  lock hash, or a preregistered complete shard-wise replay with zero unchecked
  records;
- `ALL_PATCH(REAL) == ALL_PATCH(C_COL_P)` within the frozen exact tolerance; and
- protected-access counters proving zero target/rival role and outcome reads
  before the C_COL prejoin ledger is sealed.

If the stage is missing, incompatible with P-V2, only pairwise, or merely shuffles
already sealed locks, the corrected causal chain is incomplete and reduction
must abort.

## 7. Nested inner-OOF V retraining gate

The mechanism repair must first be qualified with the historical V checkpoint so
its effects can be separated from retraining. Retraining is a second, explicitly
versioned intervention.

For each outer fold `o`, let `T_o` be its optimization population and `O_o` its
held-out population. Freeze three inner folds `I[o,1..3]` on `T_o` before any new
V fit. The split must be jointly identity-disjoint and supergroup-disjoint, with
every identity and supergroup assigned to exactly one inner fold. The existing
P inner-OOF membership may be reused only if the validator proves this exact
property and proves that each query's P lock was produced by a P checkpoint that
held out that query's identity and supergroup.

For each `(o,k)`:

- train a V candidate only on `T_o - I[o,k]`;
- evaluate checkpoint candidates only on `I[o,k]`;
- prohibit `O_o`, target/rival postjoin outputs, V123 statistics, opened/sealed
  endpoints, and per-query outcome inspection from checkpoint selection;
- retain the same repaired rootwise decoder and full-C128 controls in train and
  validation; and
- persist train/validation membership hashes, all checkpoint states, loss traces,
  raw validation margins, control margins, and protected-access counters.

The checkpoint grid, training loss, number and selection of identity-disjoint
donors, logit-scale regularizer, optimizer, and selection functional must be
frozen in a later executable authority before the 12 inner validation ledgers are
read. A single global training horizon and one shared recipe must be selected
across all 12 inner fits; per-outer-fold or per-query horizon selection is
forbidden. Ties choose the earliest checkpoint.

At minimum, the preregistered inner validation gate must require:

1. finite margins and exact candidate-reorder/pair-swap/reconstruction closure in
   all 12 inner fits;
2. no identity or supergroup leakage in either P-lock or V-fit lineage;
3. aggregate group-balanced REAL direction and mean margin above their frozen
   initialization values;
4. positive aggregate REAL-minus-valid-`C_DINO_V` mean drop, with positive drop
   in at least two of three inner folds for at least three of four outer folds;
5. no selection based on training loss alone, and no all-zero, sign-inverted, or
   unbounded-logit solution; and
6. exact fresh-versus-resume replay for the selected horizon and all four final
   outer fits.

If an inherited context initializer saw the labels of an inner-validation
identity or supergroup, that split is not a fully nested validation of the whole
model. The later authority must either provide inner-disjoint initializer lineage
or freeze the inherited representation and explicitly limit the gate to selection
of newly trained Track-R parameters. It may not call such a result fully
identity-OOF without this distinction.

After the global horizon/recipe passes, exactly four final V fits may be made on
the complete `T_o` populations. Each outer-heldout query must be scored once by
the corresponding fold checkpoint. On the existing 594, this is still only an
opened diagnostic. The recipe becomes eligible for confirmation only after it is
frozen byte-for-byte and no further change is made from the 594 outcomes.

## 8. Separation of implementation repair and learning changes

V124 must report two lineages:

```text
V124-MECH:
  historical V checkpoint + corrected C128/FULL/C_COL implementation

V124-TRAIN:
  corrected implementation + nested-selected retrained V checkpoint
```

`V124-MECH` answers whether V123's implementation changed the observed numbers.
It cannot validate a new training theory. `V124-TRAIN` answers whether the frozen
retraining policy generalizes across its nested development folds. It cannot
attribute its change solely to the engineering repair.

Candidate-binding contrast, multiple identity-disjoint training donors, scale
regularization, or a changed arm mixture are scientific training changes. If
used, their exact equations and weights require a separate preregistered version
inside `V124-TRAIN`; they may not be smuggled into `V124-MECH` or chosen from the
outer 594 margins.

## 9. Staged authority plan

No stage advances automatically:

```text
V124-D0  freeze forensic disposition and development/confirmation roles
  -> V124-E0  synthetic two-lock rejection + full-C128 donor reconstruction
              + rootwise FULL/LOCAL parity + C_COL P-V2 compatibility
  -> independent E0 validation
  -> V124-E1  fixed natural opened canary, target-free prejoin only
  -> independent E1 validation
  -> V124-MECH full opened-594 diagnostic prejoin and independent validation
  -> optional metadata join and diagnostic-only reducer
  -> V124-N0  freeze nested inner membership, checkpoint grid, objective,
              selection rule, and initializer claim boundary
  -> 12 inner fits/validation ledgers + independent nested validator
  -> one global recipe selection
  -> four final outer fits + fresh/resume validation
  -> V124-TRAIN opened-594 diagnostic only
  -> freeze byte-exact mechanism and training recipe
  -> separate authority for a new untouched confirmation endpoint
```

Each arrow requires a separate additive authority and committed independent PASS.
This draft authorizes none of them.

## 10. Fail-closed statuses and claim boundaries

The future state machine must distinguish at least:

| Condition | Required status | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| incomplete C128 donor axis, pair-swap control, FULL/LOCAL mismatch, missing C_COL, or hash drift | `V124_CORRECTED_MECHANISM_ENGINEERING_ABORT` | no scientific decision |
| nested membership or initializer leakage | `V124_NESTED_OOF_LEAKAGE_ABORT` | retraining gate invalid |
| nested gate fails | `V124_NESTED_GENERALIZATION_NO_PROMOTION` | stop retraining; not a fresh-endpoint scientific NO-GO |
| corrected opened-594 ledger closes | `V124_OPENED594_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE` | diagnostic only; scientific fields null |
| no untouched endpoint is available | `V124_CONFIRMATION_ENDPOINT_ABSENT_STOP` | no paper-level GO/NO-GO |
| untouched confirmation later passes a separately frozen authority | separately versioned GO state | only the exact preregistered conditional claim |

Even a later confirmation can establish at most candidate-bound DINO evidence
given that the natural target is already in the candidate set, plus the narrowly
matched regional/reference-scope conclusions whose controls pass. It cannot by
itself establish full-gallery recall, target absence, HOLD/SWITCH, ownership, or
deployment gain. H0/Track-H artifacts are neither inputs nor prerequisites for
the V124 mechanism repair or nested V gate.

## 11. Minimum test plan for the later implementation

The executable authority must bind tests that independently demonstrate:

1. a two-lock call to C128 `C_DINO_V` fails closed;
2. a complete C128 mapping has no fixed point, is identity-disjoint, preserves
   the native-content multiset, preserves every P lock, and does not pair-swap
   the scored anonymous members;
3. tampering with candidate position, physical row, identity, donor token, grid,
   valid mask, P-lock hash, or pair address is detected;
4. FULL invokes one decoder call per comparable root and never one union call;
5. FULL/LOCAL root order, query masks, overlap weights, denominator, reducer,
   and forward/reverse schedule are exact; only reference masks differ;
6. nonlinear sentinel data makes union-decode and rootwise-decode differ, so an
   accidental regression cannot pass by equality on a trivial fixture;
7. C_COL uses 128 pre-P donors, reruns 256 P-V2 direction locks, preserves the
   DINO axis, and changes no ALL-PATCH score;
8. shuffling sealed P locks is rejected as a fake C_COL implementation;
9. every control remains target-free before join, and independent validation
   reconstructs rather than trusts producer receipts;
10. inner folds have zero identity/supergroup overlap, validation examples never
    enter the corresponding V optimizer, one global horizon is selected, and
    fresh/resume equality closes; and
11. the 594 role registry forces diagnostic-only output and rejects any
    confirmatory/scientific status.
