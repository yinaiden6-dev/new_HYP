# B7 video opened replication V1

This experiment reuses historical B7 videos whose earlier mechanism did not
produce a valid retrieval result. Reuse is permitted; the evidence is labelled
opened exploratory replication, not untouched external confirmation.

Population: 11 high-confidence videos, 33 fixed sampled frames and 9 proposed
exact identities: DSCF0062/63, 0064/65, 0113, 0144, 0180, 0181, 0183, 0188 and
0195. The existing preliminary corrected identity/reference mapping is used
without model-based reselection. Human confirmation fields remain incomplete,
so all metrics are provisional until independently confirmed.

Consume only the immutable
`romav2_colnomic_candidate_bound_no_regret_v1_20260901` bundle. Preserve the
complete gallery, natural C128, frozen seven-parameter gate, zero threshold,
one-SWITCH rule, full-rank MRR and C_BIND. No fitting or tuning is allowed.

Report frame-level and video-clustered base/final R@1, MRR, rescue, break and
wrong-to-wrong. A positive result is supporting cross-source replication only.
